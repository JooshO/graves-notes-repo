## As Above, So Below

### Below
Below and its sub mechanics are about memory, willpower, and intent. Below pulls in.

#### Below (Space)
The below is a separate layer of existence that is most strongly manifested in areas that burn anima (soul), specifically around boilers. The space takes on the form of the memories of the space "above" it. Undead can naturally manipulate this space to some degree, reshaping it into a nightmare version of their life and using to trap and kill. The living can also exert their will to change the below, sometimes just to allow them to strike their foes but other times to greater effect. To do so, they make a Below Check and gain a stack of Below.

#### Below Check
A being makes a below check to influence or otherwise shape the Below space. When you enter the below, all three mental saves are available to make Below checks. As you use them, the difficulty escalates and you can no longer use them. This ends up like the following:

| DC  | Role                       |
| --- | -------------------------- |
| 5   | Any mental save            |
| 10  | Any yet unused mental save |
| 15  | Last remaining mental save |
| 20  | Any mental save            |
#### Below Stacks
When entering the below, and as a consequence of making Below Checks and some other actions, you gain Below Stacks. 
If you exit the Below (by physically leaving it or by being forced out), you take 1d4 damage per Below Stack, then they are all removed. Conversely, if your Below Stack hits zero, you leave the Below immediately.
If you hit zero HP while you have any stacks of below, you are Fading.

##### Uses
You can take on 10 stacks of below to negate a usage of Legendary Resistance
You can spend one stack of below when healing someone to remove Fading

#### Fading
While you are fading you are Dying, but not unconscious. Your attacks deal Decay damage (double check this one).

#### Decay
Most commonly granted by fading or by [[Martin Hale]]'s ability, Decay damage frequently deals extra damage to undead or bypasses resistances.
