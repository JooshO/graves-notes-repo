# Thrill - Exulted

You may note that thrill damage counts as **Decay** for the purposes of overcoming resistances and immunities. 

When you miss while having thrill active, you may instead of losing all your thrill, take damage equal to your current thrill. If this damage would reduce you to zero hit points, you instead gain **Fading**. 

When you hit a creature and deal 10 or more damage from thrill dice alone, you may cause them to either enter or exit the **Below**. You may follow the target either entering or exiting the **Below** without taking damage from **Below** when you exit. A **Below** space does not need to exist for this ability to activate. You may instead have the target, yourself, and every willing creature you can see (and select) enter or exit the **Below**. Each of the selected creatures do not take damage from exiting **Below**, but you immediately take a number of **Below** equal to the selected creatures and take damage as normal. The target still takes **Below** damage as normal. 

While **Fading**, your thrill dice become d8s.
