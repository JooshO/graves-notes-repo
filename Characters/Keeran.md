“Kas, Am Tyr, Wren, Am Reman, Am Peral, Iyun, forgive me this violence I am about to commit” - Litany Before Battle

Low ranking fist in the Gunmetal crew with a couple of people and a block or two under his purview. Loosely superstitious, believes in a patheonic collection of older gods and quotes/invokes litanies and prayers somewhat frequently. He has a collection of old fables and stories he likes to tell. Keeran is an easy, charismatic, lead from the front type who prefers to solve problems by cutting them up into smaller pieces - some times more literally than others.

“This is my home; I will flee no further. My enemies will turn back, or they will come unto me and be broken” - Litany of the Gate (Am Peral)

Keeran likes grilled cheese and fried potato wedges, encourages and donates to buskers on his turf, and has settled blood debts with his fists. He is a pleasant, passionate, creative person who would die for his people and kill for his friends - and has done the latter.

Keeran struggles with imposter syndrome - a part of him can’t reconcile his personality and actions.

Keeran owes a favor to a couple of merchants with weight in a (barely) allied gang.

Keeran is owed a favor by John Wyl, owner of the Old Oak further uptown.

Keeran can frequently be found at the Heavy Lids Tavern on his block, organizing, carousing, or killing time.

Keeran is one of the few members in his Crew who is debtless. He mainly sticks around because his skill set matches the job well (read: I’m good at leading people and putting them away, and I’m not sure what else) and he is loyal to his subordinates.

“Let my fire burn out. Let my worries flow away like water. Let the winds at my back leave me and the weights on my body fall free. Let me be empty, that I may become perfect” - Litany of Emptiness
