
## Litany Before Battle
Kas, Am Tyr, Wren, Am Reman, Am Peral, Iyun, forgive me this violence I am about to commit

## Litany of the Flame
Am Tyr, guide my heart with warm red flame to hold fast against the dark.
Iyun, grant my hands the cold white flame that I may burn away the night.

## Litany of Emptiness
Let my fire burn out. Let my worries flow away like water. Let the winds at my back leave me and the weights on my body fall free. Let me be empty, that I may become perfect

## Litany of Sanctuary from the End
Old Man Autumn, guide and keep me at the ending of things and the edge of the world.

## Litany of the Forge
Am Tyr, make of my soul a weapon that I may lay low tyrants and fiends.

## Litany of the Gate
This is my home; I will flee no further. My enemies will turn back, or they will come unto me and be broken.

## Litany of the Guardian
This is a place of safety. You will do no more harm here. Come unto me and be broken
