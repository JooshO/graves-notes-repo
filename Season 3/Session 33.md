Shit's gone strange. Police sirens echo through the city. Tarazed is off (Jasper).

The Crew pushes into the building to take cover. Top building is like an observatory. Spiral staircase twists upwards to glass ceilings and various rooms. Jasper notes that it crawls with Arcanotech.

Keeran speaks out to the Brigade to try to break their assault. Some of the Brigade are already dead - they do not flinch. Keeran reaches some of the living, but not enough to make a difference.

One final car rolls up - a wide police van. Out of it steps an old man - Sahat in a bullet proof vest.
Keeran gives instructions and steps into the below to begin building out a weaponized domain.

We have one sewer access behind the building and there are 4 in and about the horde.

We scheme for an extended period. Step 1 - exit through sewer to flank Sahat. Step 2 - attack in the Below. We creep in behind Sahat, sneaking past the cops, and go Below. In this memory - sand and obsidian. Sahat the Pharoah. Roll initiative.

Keeran casts Bless (Litany of the Flame) and snaps to behind.
Tarazed casts mirror image and closes to complete the flank.
Sahat legendary actions to try to punch Keeran but Keeran dodges away.
Theo tries to cast Hideous Laughter but Sahat saves.
Sahat drifts as a sandstorm to Wilward.
Sahat summons a glaive and lays into Wilward, cursing him.
Wilward lights him up with Hellish Rebuke, burning him bad.
Keeran closes and smashes him with a burning punch then rips out his heart.

The zombies can't use guns but they will try to eat us - the main crowd cannibalizes itself but several attack us. Two hit Theo. Keeran takes 10 bludgeoning from another two. Some clip Jasper. Tales of the Traveler teleports us to the sewer and to safety.
