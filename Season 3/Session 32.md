Operation Wasteland Winds
10/11/1635

We roll up on the ornate central building with an orrery atop it. Violence and gunshots ripple across the city. Statues of great navigators are arrayed outside. Out from the doors hobbles an old man - [[Constantine Antelivich]]. He's whole and bursts into ghastly green smoke.

Keeran casts Bless on himself and dips his eyes into the below - sees a bit of the true form of Constantine.
Theo casts Dissonant Whispers and Constantine is able to shrug most of it off. Theo pulls from his book.
Constantine laughs, shifting into lime/neon green smoke. He moves over a statue and it wakes up - rapier and sextant, a grand navigator.
Wilward activates protection from evil and good.
Tarazed casts his vow of enmity on the structure then lays into it. The statue begins to crumble and it can't stay up. Constantine pops up and Tarazed smacks him again (42 to base Constantine). The veins near Tarazed's eyes start to go black and his skin clings to his bones.

Keeran goes Below - "CONSTANTINE! I REMEMBER YOU BROKEN!". He sees the many faced mafioso with a bullet hole in his chest. He rips open the domain to bring in his own. Constantine's domain is the Mafia house with jazz and smoke, Keeran's is the fighting pit with sand spiraling out and his own statues. Am Reman splits open the house to introduce Keeran's domain.
Below 1

Theo reaches out to Constantine and casts Psychic Lance which Keeran sees in the Below. Constantine crit saves.

Keeran wins a domain round with Constantine and the fighting pit expands. He disappears in the real world temporarily.

Tarazed does not like Keeran tearing into the domain. Theo and Tarazed enter the below.
Wilward throws up illusory statues to distract and deceive Constantine.

Tarazed burns at the below by being there. He lands a hit and becomes yet more corrupted. Constantine isn't looking perfect. These strikes are taking a toll. Another blow lands. 

Theo and Theo alone sees a veiled figure looking over Tarazed as he strikes. Each time, she winces. She always wears a veil. She is more in mourning than she was before. The winces are for Tarazed. Another blow lands.

Constantine tries to eject Tarazed - 4 below or leave. Tarazed choses to stay.

Keeran rips further into the domain - on the border of victory. Tarazed recognizes he is on the border of being annihilated without vengeance. Keeran is now below 3.

Theo notices that Tarazed is looking undead. Theo places Tarazed as being a revenant and knows that Keeran has to be stopped or else Tarazed will die. Theo alerts Keeran. Keeran does not like this. He lets Theo out. Theo alerts Wilward and Wilward goes below.

Constantine fails to push back and jumps into an illusory statue that Wilward created and all in all gets nothing. 

Wilward tries to give Tarazed the poison as a honey stick after some yelling. Tarazed falls for it and is dying.

Constantine has 108hp

Tarazed swings at Constantine. Constantine is down to 56 hp. Tarazed misses Wilward and hits Constantine. 

Keeran steps to Tarazed - lariat, axe kick to drop him and kill Tarazed/free Jasper. He asks Keeran "why" in betrayal as he fades away. Keeran points to Constantine - **kill**.

Theo steps into the below and summons a ghostly fencer that runs Constantine through, cutting the tether that holds him to this earth.

Keeran steps onto a plinth. "Constantine is broken. Your cause is lost. Forfeit or die.". Greenbacks are disheartened and the Crew fights with renewed vigor.

We see Tarazed, face down. The Veiled Lady is here. She puts her hand on Tarazed shoulder. We can see the pale face of Shari, the Lady of Pain, smiling. Jasper feels the pain of the fighting coming back to his limbs. There is still a grudge there, but it is far less monstrous. His arm is very much missing. Jasper returns and promptly pukes. He looks up to the face of the shrouded figure. She says "It's been a long time since I've seen someone come back from that place. And though many who worshipped me followed the line of judgement, vengeance, and revenance, those followers only sought retribution." She kneels. "The true power I offer is Absolution". Retribution crumples in on itself and attaches itself to Jasper's arm as a clawed hand crackling with electric power. Retribution is now Absolution.

Jasper is an armorer artificer. Tankier and with more support-ish options than Tarazed. Jasper can make sending stones.

Keeran is victorious, having nearly overwritten the memory of the God Soul. He feels the wholeness of a battle well won. And then we hear police sirens.
