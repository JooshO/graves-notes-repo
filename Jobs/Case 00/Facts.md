### Initial Entry - 6/4/1635

One Haunting (detailed in the [[Briefing]] for Job 00) occurred at [[Wight's Fabrotorium and Machinery Shop]]. The Shade causing the Haunting developed from the soul of [[Jeoorge Oafson]].

The following is information derived from telepathic communications with the Shade.
[[Jeoorge Oafson]] was an engineer who worked for [[Bulwark Wight]]. 
He was a graduate of [[Meyrin University]], likely Ignotin, Coffswon, Saint's, or Meyrin.
He has a strong emotional connection to the children of [[Oaf's Orphanage]].
He has a strong emotional connection to the works of [[Phillip Drinker]].
[[Phillip Drinker]] is a patriarch of the Drinker family, and an inventor from the early days of the Free City. His relevant work here is a currently unknown paper cited to "P. Drinker et al".
There is a connection to the term [[Iron Lung]]. Connected to this term is an idea of engine blocks and a human placed inside a construct with many tubes and wires.

The most connected vision is as follows:
Jeoorge hunched over a table etching a metal piece for cutting later. He turns to look at a large, intricate blueprint. The observer sees two metal figures that Jeoorge seems to be working on or creating. (*Interpretation: figures as in humanoid? Automata?*). While reviewing the blueprint, Jeoorge seems to come to a realization. He tears the blueprint off the wall and shreds it into small pieces. Similarly, he destroys the metal piece he was etching. Jeoorge runs his hands through his hair, visibly distressed. He turns and views the door to his room, where he sees a man wearing a upper class three-piece suit with a watch. The man is balding on top with hair on the sides and a mustache. The man turns and leaves. (*Speculation: this man is [[Bulwark Wight]]? To be confirmed from description*).
